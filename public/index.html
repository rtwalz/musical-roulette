musical roulette

How well do you know your friends’ music taste? Every round plays a short clip from someone’s Spotify top tracks. Guess whose song it is before time runs out.

If several players like the same song, the person who ranks it highest is the answer. The faster you guess correctly, the more points you earn.

Log in with Spotify, then create a game or join with a friend’s six-digit code. Play together in one room or turn on sound on your own device.
